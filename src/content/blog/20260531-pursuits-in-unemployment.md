## Giving Watercolor Another Go

Fresh off of my trip to Japan and loaded with vacation pictures, I decided to take another stab at watercolor.

![watercolor painting of toji temple in Kyoto](/images/blog/toji-watercolor.webp)

Painting of Toji Temple in KyotoIn response to short-form video and AI-generated content, I have developed a newfound appreciation for traditional media. Watercolor is the easy choice. It doesn’t require ventilation and special binders like oil paints do, and it doesn’t pose the environmental/wastewater issues of pouring paint water down the drain like acrylic paints do.

I have a complicated relationship with watercolor. I prefer to move through life as a perfectionist, so my usual medium of choice is color pencil. It offers precision, erasability, and resistance to smudging.

![](/images/blog/fuji-watercolor.webp "my friend in front of Mt Fuji")

Watercolor is the opposite in almost every way. The paper warps, and the colors bleed into each other. It’s hard to come back from laying down too much pigment. (As a disclaimer, I’m also using some pretty terrible paper. I am aware that nicer papers may let you lift color more easily.)

Nevertheless, I persisted. It’s lame to let the fear of mediocrity prevent you from trying in the first place. Since I do not do this for a living, I have the luxury of focusing on the process rather than the result, and to push through and try to learn something rather than give up prematurely.

![](/images/blog/osaka-watercolor.webp)

Here’s to everything I've learned and painted thus far, and to many more learnings and paintings to come!

![](/images/blog/osaka-watercolor-final.webp "Osaka Castle")

## On Hinge

I created a Hinge profile for the plot. I’ve never dated before, and they say there’s no better time to pursue new life experiences than when you’re incredibly unemployed.

I’m quite proud of my profile actually.

Despite having been on Hinge for less than a month, I would confidently and perhaps arrogantly say that a lot of the people on Hinge are not putting their best foot forward with their profiles.

**A list of my Hinge pet peeves in no particular order:**

* **Opening with a terrible selfie**. Why would you put a horrendously backlit photo where your face is barely visible at the top?
* **“Looking for someone funny”** Please demonstrate your own sense of humor on your profile a little, because I promise not all humor is created equal.
* **“Loves music/going to concerts”** So do I, but let’s please specify the type of music. Twenty One Pilots and Kpop are not the same.
* **“Loves going to movies/reading books”** Same as above.
* **Answering prompts with < 5 words.** You’re wasting precious text real estate here.
* **“Hidden gem”** We live in NYC.

It’s a short list because I predominantly see profiles from women, a subgroup known for having better-than-average Hinge profiles. My friends who are on gay and bisexual Hinge have more to say on this topic.

**A list of Hinge pet peeves from my bisexual friend who sees mostly straight men:**

* **All or almost all of the photos are group photos.** Please have the friends in your photos help you take some flattering solo shots.
* **“This year, I want to travel”** "ok."
* **“The craziest thing I did was travel”** "ok."
* **love language prompts.** "More so for men, the love language is always physical touch lol"
* **All photos of a man involve a hat/head covering.  "**Let's see the hair density situation, please"

**A list of Hinge pet peeves from my gay friend:**

* **"im looking for someone who is kind, funny, smart etc."** "wow how informative"
* **“Give me travel tips for”** “This is one of the worst prompts and Hinge should get rid of it”
* **Excessive shirtless photos**
