## Potato Cake Saga

To commemorate my 27th birthday, I organized a potato party in my new home. I invited guests to bring a potato-based dish, snacks, and/or drinks, and then forced them to play a custom-made Kahoot on random potato trivia. Winners were bestowed fresh potatoes from my local supermarket.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

You can play the Kahoot [here](https://create.kahoot.it/share/potato-jirthday-kahoot/d00bad51-cecc-4000-ae79-8ca468819ace) by choosing the “play solo” option.

Days prior to the event, I toiled away across two days with a small committee of friends to bake a potato-shaped cake. I had never baked a cake before, but my friend who had “a lot of experience watching cake boss” persuaded me into doing it. She had a vision inspired by an image she had seen online.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

Baking the cake layers was the easy part by design. We used two boxes of [Betty Crocker box mix](https://www.bettycrocker.com/products/betty-crocker-baking-and-cake-mixes/betty-crocker-lower-sugar-cake-mix-yellow), and the only hiccup we faced was when I forgot to temper the eggs while adding the hot melted butter, causing the eggs to curdle. You learn something new every day.

The actual problems began with the swiss meringue buttercream, something I didn’t even know existed prior to the start of this project. We followed a section in [this ube cake recipe](https://theunlikelybaker.com/ube-cake-filipino-purple-yam-cake/) to make a purple ube swiss meringue buttercream.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

I did not yet have an AC unit in my dining room/kitchen, and it was 80 degrees that day. It was too hot in my kitchen, and the butter had melted way past room temperature by the time we added it to our meringue. I learned later that when butter has melted too far, it ruins the emulsion of the butter and its ability to hold shape. This combined with the heat kind of made it too runny, and no amount of whipping seemed to help.👎

Panicking, we consulted the internet, which told us to stick it in the fridge and not to worry too much. In the end, we got it cold and solid enough that we were able to apply it to the cake. But if the cake was out of the fridge for more than 15 minutes, the buttercream would start to melt again. This was good enough for us.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)


![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

While writing this blog post, I spent some time on cooking/baking subreddits to understand exactly what went wrong, and how we could’ve done better. The tl;dr is that if you mess up the meringue in any way, it’s genuinely joever and you have to start again. But anything that happens after that, including adding butter that is too soft, is usually salvageable. In our case, the answer may have been to add more cold butter and [“beat it into submission”](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskBaking/comments/1crxh8b/comment/l419qyg/) and also to [put bags of frozen vegetables](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cooking/comments/jqbmlz/comment/gbma1j7/) around the outside of the bowl to keep the temperature of the mixture down.Obviously, it’s almost impossible to know all this the first time you make it, but that’s just what life is about.

The second major hurdle was the white modeling chocolate. For the outer layer of the potato cake, we had chosen modeling chocolate over fondant because of reports (also from Reddit) that it tasted better. Though I’ve never had fondant, I would wager that modeling chocolate, which is just corn syrup and fat, is probably more palatable than fondant, which supposedly tastes like play-doh.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)
It was more expensive than I expected!!

Our plan was to work brown food coloring into the modeling chocolate until potato-colored, roll it out, drape it over the cake, and shape it to the cake.

The ube-frosted cake was **very large** and **dangerously turd-shaped** at this point, and I was afraid that if we went too far with the food coloring, we would have a turd cake on our hands.


The main issues once again ended up being heat and inexperience. None of us had ever worked with modeling chocolate before. As one of my friends was kneading the food coloring in, the oils from the modeling chocolate were leaking everywhere because of the heat.

The lump of modeling chocolate was coming out suspiciously flesh-colored. I was so worried about the layer turning out turd-colored that I didn’t even consider the possibility of it coming out flesh-colored.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

My friend joked that it looked like the “moisturize me” image.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

At around 9PM, we learned that freshly-kneaded modeling chocolate was too soft to drape over the cake. And thus I stuck the lump of chocolate in the fridge and sent everyone home.

The next morning on the day of the party, by some luck and googling, I was able to roll it out alone and put it over the cake. Though it still looked like a flesh lump, it was heartening knowing that I would have something presentable for my friends later.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

The real magic happened when we spread cocoa powder on it, turning it into a real russet potato. It looked nothing like the picture we were inspired by, but a potato is a potato.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

My guests were delighted. It looked like cake and tasted good. Nobody actually ate the outer layer, but this did not bother me.

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

I received feedback that my birthday celebrations tend to be too carb-heavy, which I cannot deny. My 26th birthday party involved guests bringing in a type of bread from the [List of Breads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_breads) plus meats, cheeses, spreads, and fruits for a potluck charcuterie in the park.If anyone has ideas for themed potlucks that are less carb heavy, please email me or DM me. I’ve always considered myself more of an execution guy than an ideas guy.

## Job Search Progress

It is no secret that I am still unemployed, but this does not bother me. As of June, I am actively looking for employment.At the end of April, I moved into a new place and then spent all of May doing miscellaneous chores. I only moved seven blocks away, but moving takes a lot out of you no matter the distance.

Since seriously entering the job market, I have gotten to the 2nd and 3rd round of interviews at 2 companies, but ultimately did not clear them. Perhaps I underestimated the expectations that a company would have for someone with “Software Development Engineer II” on their resume. 💀

![](IMAGE\_PLACEHOLDER)

So I have been hitting the books between my unemployment activities, spending time learning technology that I have never used so I can talk about it like I have. It’s a little nostalgic because studying for system design interviews reminds me of cramming for finals.

I’m taking it slow and focusing on learning though because not enough time has passed for me to have forgotten the horrors of corporate employment. I suppose I will keep applying to jobs and trying my best.
